Grammar

Verb To Be
Pessoa+am / is / are+...
I am shy.
She is outgoing.
They are friendly.
I→am
He, She→is
You, We, They→are
Present Simple
Pessoa+Verbo
I work every day.
He works every day.
I, You, We, They→Verbo
He, She→Verbo + S
Pessoa+don't / doesn't+Verbo
I don't cook.
He doesn't cook.
How Often?
Pessoa+usually+Verbo
I always drink coffee.
She never sleeps late.
Pessoa+am / is / are+usually
I am usually late.
Com verbo normal, o advérbio vem antes do verbo. Com To Be, vem depois.
